## Legal Considerations

### Contracts & Agreements

**Essential Documents:**
```
1. Master Service Agreement (MSA)
   - Scope of work
   - Payment terms
   - Liability limits
   - Termination clauses
   - IP ownership

2. Statement of Work (SOW)
   - Specific deliverables
   - Timeline
   - Acceptance criteria
   - Change order process

3. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
   - Mutual vs one-way
   - Exclusions (public info, prior knowledge)
   - Duration (typically 2-5 years)

4. Independent Contractor Agreement
   - Not an employee
   - Tax responsibilities
   - No benefits
```

**Key Clauses to Include:**

| Clause | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **IP Assignment** | Who owns the code you write? (Usually client, but negotiate) |
| **Liability Cap** | Limit your exposure (usually 1x project value) |
| **Indemnification** | Protection against third-party claims |
| **Payment Terms** | Net 15-30, milestone-based, deposit required |
| **Kill Fee** | Compensation if project cancelled mid-way |
| **Non-Compete** | Be careful - limit scope and duration |
| **Warranty Period** | 30-90 days of bug fixes included |
