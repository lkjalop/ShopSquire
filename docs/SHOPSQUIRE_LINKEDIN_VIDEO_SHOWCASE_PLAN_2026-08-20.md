# ShopSquire LinkedIn Video Showcase Plan

Date: 2026-08-20

## Positioning

Most procurement-agent demos stop at a recommendation. This showcase demonstrates
what must remain correct after a buyer changes their mind: typed state, temporal
authority, purpose-bounded research, inventory protection, allocation, and explicit
commerce authority.

The central line is:

> Language proposes. Deterministic state decides.

## Primary product video: 60-75 seconds

1. **0-6 seconds — Hook**
   - Open on the Procurement Decision Lab.
   - Caption: "What happens when a buyer changes a 60-laptop order mid-conversation?"
2. **6-18 seconds — First turn**
   - Show 40 laptops to Sydney, 20 to Perth, Unreal Engine/CAD/simulation workloads,
     AUD 220,000, at least 30 within four days, and a seven-day cover floor.
3. **18-28 seconds — Revision**
   - Show the buyer moving five Perth units to Sydney.
   - Emphasize the atomic revision: Sydney 45, Perth 15, total still 60, all other
     constraints retained.
4. **28-38 seconds — Temporal authority**
   - Show the Sydney timezone, interpretation instant, resolved UTC deadline,
     calendar version, and confidence.
   - Caption: "Ambiguous time expressions fail closed with one clarification."
5. **38-49 seconds — Purpose and consent**
   - Contrast the workload-fit query with the location-aware logistics query.
   - Show zero network calls before authorization.
6. **49-61 seconds — Allocation**
   - Show 41 of 60 allocated by the deadline, the 30-unit early-arrival threshold met,
     the seven-day origin cover preserved, and a 19-unit supplier shortfall retained
     as a proposal only.
7. **61-72 seconds — Adjudicated truth**
   - Show research complete, evidence accepted, freshness current, decision
     conditional, and commerce authority none.
   - End on the sealed certificate hash and the central line.

## Engineering follow-up video: 90 seconds

Use a split screen with the browser certificate on the left and selected code or
tests on the right. Explain five decisions:

1. Natural language produces bounded typed patches; it does not directly mutate
   commercial state.
2. Time is interpreted against an explicit instant and IANA timezone, then persisted
   with provenance and ambiguity status.
3. Research relevance is separate from authorization to perform network work.
4. Allocation conserves demand while protecting origin stock cover and leaves the
   supplier shortfall non-authoritative.
5. Every panel consumes one adjudication projection, preventing contradictory UI
   claims about evidence, freshness, decisions, and commerce authority.

## Expertise to make visible

- State-machine and reducer design for conversational systems.
- Temporal semantics, timezone handling, provenance, and fail-closed ambiguity.
- Consent and authority boundaries for agentic network and commerce actions.
- Inventory allocation, conservation invariants, and protected stock policy.
- Connector enrollment, tenant isolation, watermarks, and ingestion health.
- Evidence engineering: deterministic fixtures, revision-bound observations,
  production-shaped browser tests, and sealed artifacts.
- Product judgment: translating backend authority into buyer-visible truth.

## Claims boundary

Safe claims:

- Deterministic, fixture-backed two-turn browser certificate.
- Zero pre-authorization network calls in the certified scenario.
- No cart mutation or supplier send in the certified scenario.
- Enrolled connector ingestion contract demonstrated with reviewed fixtures.
- Browser artifact and certificate are sealed and reproducible.

Do not claim yet:

- Live WMS, carrier, pricing, or supplier traffic was used in this certificate.
- Autonomous purchasing or a production supplier order occurred.
- A paid external provider was called.
- The full repository release suite is green until infrastructure-backed gates and
  the recorded baseline failures are closed.
