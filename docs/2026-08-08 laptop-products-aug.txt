INGESTION NOTES FOR THE PIPELINE

1. Store MPN as a first-class field. Every one of these 9 publishes one. Your existing
   synthetic catalog has no MPN field — that is the single highest-leverage schema change.
2. Three freshness classes, not one:
     specs         long TTL   (change only on revision)
     warranty/OS   long TTL
     price         MINUTES    (see the OMEN record)
     availability  real-time, and PER LOCATION where published
3. Extract from prose when the structured table is empty (Legion 9i, Zephyrus Duo) and
   stamp observed_source so confidence can be lowered downstream.
4. Never resolve a CONFLICT by picking or averaging. Carry both and mark it.
5. Never copy an attribute across SKUs sharing a GPU name. The two RTX 5090 Laptop
   machines here differ, and only one publishes TGP.
6. Retailer category tags ("Gaming PC: Yes", "Product Use: General, Gaming") are
   merchandising, not fit evidence. Keep them out of the requirement ledger.
