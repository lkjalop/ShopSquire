## TDD and what green means

For behavioral repairs: reproduce the intended failure on the parent, retain the business oracle, make a coherent minimal change, then run positive and neighboring negative controls on a reconstructed source. Missing imports/files, malformed harnesses, absent prerequisites and observer-induced retention are not application reds. Fixture corrections must preserve assertions and prove they exercise the intended application configuration. Preserve failed and invalid attempts with their reason.

A **named gate is green** only for its recorded source, installed dependencies, actor/tenant/prerequisites and complete intended assertions when native exit is zero, resource/latency limits hold and required cases are not skipped. Original guards remain 5000 handles, 2 GiB RSS, at least 1 GiB host headroom and the profile's recorded deadlines; none was raised to pass. A ready draft does not grant send authority. Remembered quantity does not mean completed cited analysis. Safe denial does not demonstrate positive delivery.

**Release green** needs one integrated frozen installation to pass every required code, migration, dependency, browser, security, service, privacy/recovery, restore/rollback and endurance gate. Every partition needs before/after source hashes. Independently green cohorts, historical observations and isolated process partitions do not compose that certificate. No overall completion percentage is reported.
