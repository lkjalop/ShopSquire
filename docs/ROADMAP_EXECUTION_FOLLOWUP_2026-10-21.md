## TDD and what green means

For each repair, establish a meaningful failing behavior on the parent, preserve the intended oracle, make the smallest coherent change, then run the positive and neighboring negative controls on an independently reconstructed candidate. Missing imports, malformed harnesses, absent prerequisites, a reused Response or a test that never reaches its business assertion are not valid application reds. Retain those attempts as invalid or prerequisite failures rather than erasing them. Never widen deadlines, raise resource budgets, remove business assertions or invent stock/evidence to claim a pass.

A **named gate is green** only for its identified source, installation, actor, tenant and prerequisite profile when the intended assertions execute, native exit is zero, required cases are not skipped and resource/latency limits hold. Browser green also requires the intended business result and correct authority. A ready email preview is not an authorized send; remembered facts are not completed market analysis; safe denial is not positive task delivery. Native assertion success followed by resource termination is not a green process.

**Release green** requires one frozen integrated installation to pass all required code, migration, dependency, browser, security, native-service, privacy/recovery, restore/rollback and endurance gates. Tests from different versions or process partitions do not compose that certificate. No overall completion percentage is reported.
