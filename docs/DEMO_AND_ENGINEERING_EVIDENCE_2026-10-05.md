# What ShopSquire can demonstrate

This is an AI-assisted fictional electronics shop. Products imported from the inventory document are unverified snapshots. Fictional requirement evidence and compatibility relationships are explicitly labelled. Messages, purchases and payment effects remain simulated or blocked. This document does not certify production readiness.

## Engineering experience you can describe

Use first-person statements that reflect your actual role in directing and reviewing the AI-assisted work:

> I developed an AI-assisted commerce prototype using test-first behavioral checks, real-API browser journeys and isolated synthetic data. I used failed evaluations to identify integration defects, then verified repairs at service, database and UI boundaries. I kept model proposals separate from evidence, authorization and transaction rules.

- **TDD:** concrete red/green examples include quantity corrections, deadline/budget checks, catalogue filter metadata, erasure under backend failure, egress restoration and logging continuity. Explain the failing assertion, the smallest repair and the regression evidence. Not every existing module was developed with TDD.
- **Testing:** distinguish unit/component tests, transaction and authorization integration tests, real Redis/PostgreSQL checks, Playwright clickthroughs and duration-based reliability tests. A green targeted test is not a green platform.
- **Harness engineering:** fresh fixtures, generated identities, bounded model configuration, owned process cleanup, immutable source checkpoints, durable effect fingerprints, explicit skips and machine-readable receipts make failures reproducible.
- **Loop engineering:** a model proposes an interpretation; validated facts update revision-bound state; downstream retrieval and effects recheck that state. The engineering loop preserves failed evidence, diagnoses a boundary, adds a regression and reruns connected behavior.
- **CI/CD:** you can discuss workflow configuration, local CI-equivalent checks, clean setup work and a same-schema rollback exercise. Do not describe current remote CI or automated production deployment as proven: neither was observed for this working tree.

Avoid claiming production security, completed commerce autonomy, a superior model, eight-hour stability or independently validated forecast improvements. The synthetic forecasting challengers did not justify replacing the baseline.

## Remaining showcase gates

The most valuable next proof is a fully connected, reviewed supplier selection through approved simulated purchasing and recovery, including current quantity, freight, MOQ and deadline consequences. Remaining work also includes concurrent erase/write races, broader category and adversarial coverage, independent benchmark labels, sustained varied-model/image testing, and release automation. The agnostic core should continue to enforce identity, revision, freshness and transaction invariants; product vocabulary and workload facts belong in profiles and evidence adapters.
